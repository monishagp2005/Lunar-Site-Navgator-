About this planner

Selene brings local horizon geometry, lighting availability, and Earth line of sight into one mission view. Move the local-sol slider to inspect how the Sun and Earth shift relative to the site horizon.

Prototype data: candidate coordinates, terrain masks, and availability estimates are illustrative. Replace them with validated terrain and ephemeris products before using results for mission decisions.
